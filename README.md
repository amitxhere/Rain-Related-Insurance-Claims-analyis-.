# Rain-Related-Insurance-Claims-analyis-.
## Project Introduction

This project focuses on analyzing insurance claims related to rainfall events. The primary objective is to identify trends, patterns, and insights from historical insurance claim data to support risk assessment and decision-making. By leveraging Power BI, this dashboard visualizes key metrics, enabling stakeholders to quickly understand the impact of rain-related events on insurance claims across various dimensions such as claim type, status, and region.

The dataset includes information on claim amounts, claim types, policy types, claim status, states, and the year of claim. The dashboard is interactive, allowing users to filter data using slicers for Year, Policy Type, and Claim Status.

## Dashboard Overview & Key Visualizations

## 1.Total Claim Amount by Rain Category

Displays the total monetary value of claims grouped by different rain categories (e.g., light, moderate, heavy).

Helps in understanding which rain intensity results in higher financial exposure for insurance companies.

## 2.Distribution of Claims by Type

Shows the count of claims categorized by claim type (e.g., property damage, vehicle damage).

Useful to identify the most common types of claims arising due to rain events.

## 3.Distribution of Claims by Status

Visualizes claims by their current status (e.g., approved, pending, rejected).

Allows tracking the resolution efficiency and pending claims backlog.

## 4.Total Claim Amount by State

Illustrates the total claim amount across different states.

Enables geographical analysis of rain-related insurance claims and identification of high-risk regions.

## 5.Count of Claims by Type and Status

Provides a combined view showing how many claims of each type fall under each status.

Assists in evaluating operational efficiency and claim resolution trends.

## 6.Fraud vs Non-Fraud Claims Percentage

Depicts the proportion of fraudulent claims compared to genuine claims.

Crucial for understanding the risk of fraudulent activities and implementing preventive measures.

## 7.Average Claim Amount by Claim Type

Shows the average monetary value of claims for each claim type.

Helps identify which types of claims are more costly on average.

## Interactive Features

The dashboard includes slicers for enhanced interactivity:

Year – Filter claims based on specific years.

Policy Type – View claims corresponding to specific insurance policies.

Claim Status – Analyze claims according to their approval or rejection status.

These slicers allow users to drill down into specific datasets for more targeted analysis.

## Key Insights

Certain rain categories (e.g., heavy rain) contribute significantly to total claim amounts.

Property and vehicle damage are the most common claim types during rain events.

Some states experience higher claim amounts, indicating regional vulnerability.

Fraudulent claims are a small but important percentage, highlighting the need for vigilant checks.

Average claim amounts vary by claim type, suggesting areas where insurance companies face higher financial risk.
